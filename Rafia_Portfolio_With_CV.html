A clear process

From website audit to measurable next steps.

A simple, transparent workflow keeps the work focused on issues that matter.

STEP 01

Audit

Review crawling, indexing, on-page basics, speed, and the website’s current search data.

STEP 02

Prioritize

Group issues by impact and effort, then agree on scope and what should be addressed first.

STEP 03

Implement

Make agreed updates on the website or provide clear, actionable instructions for your developer.

STEP 04

Review

Check the completed work and track available Search Console and performance signals over time.
